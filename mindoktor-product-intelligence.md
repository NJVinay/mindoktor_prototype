# Min Doktor — Product Intelligence Report
**What it does, how it serves patients, what it beats, what it lacks**

---

## What Min Doktor Actually Is (Product Model)

Min Doktor is NOT just a website — it is a blended digital-physical primary care system
with three distinct product layers:

| Layer | What it is | How patient accesses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Digital consultation** | Async chat with licensed clinicians | App / guides.mindoktor.se (BankID) |
| **Vaccination clinic network** | Nurse-led drop-in clinics | Walk-in at 60+ ICA Maxi / Apotek Hjärtat sites |
| **Public health center (BVC)** | Full primary care, pediatrics | Listed patients, Nyköping only |

---

## Features & Patient-Facing Capabilities

### Core Consultation Engine
- **24/7 asynchronous chat** — no scheduled appointments, no phone queues
- **Multidisciplinary clinician pool**: doctor, nurse, midwife, psychologist,
  physiotherapist, sexologist, dietitian — all under one platform
- **Structured symptom questionnaire** routes case to the right clinician type
  before any human is involved (built-in triage at intake)
- **Prescription issuance** — e-prescriptions sent directly to pharmacy (Apotek Hjärtat)
- **Prescription renewal** — dedicated fast-track flow (/receptfornyelse/)
- **Lab test & imaging requests** — can be issued digitally, no physical GP visit needed
- **Referrals** — issues referrals to specialist care when needed
- **Children from 6 months** — one of very few digital providers with pediatric scope
- **No listed-patient requirement** — any Swedish resident can access, not locked to region
- **Response SLA ~1 hour** — stated internally, not shown to patient pre-consultation

### Condition Scope (55+ conditions across 11 categories)
Cold/respiratory, Skin, Women's health, Stomach/GI, Muscles & joints,
Men's health, Mental health, Lifestyle (weight, alcohol, nicotine),
Other (headache, thyroid, contraceptives), Children, Allergy

### Physical Services (Vaccination Clinics)
- Travel vaccines, TBE, shingles (Zostavax), varicella, HPV, flu
- Blood pressure measurement (100 SEK)
- Ear irrigation (500 SEK)
- Wound care / nurse assessment (300 SEK)
- Mobile "vaccination buses" for high-demand areas
- Drop-in model — no booking required at most sites

### Digital Tools
| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Digital vaccination card** | Auto-logs vaccines from Min Doktor clinics; accessible in patient account; free |
| **Journalen (health blog)** | SEO content + self-care guides + service explainers |
| **Condition library** | 55+ condition pages linking directly into consultation flows |
| **App (iOS + Android)** | Full consultation, messaging, prescription tracking, vaccination history |
| **Women's health hub** | Dedicated entry (/kvinnohalsa/) — midwife, contraception, PMS, UTI |
| **Weight loss / dietitian hub** | Dedicated entry — dietitian chat, weight loss programme |
| **Birthmark check** | Photo-based skin assessment (/fodelsemarkeskontroll/) |

### Business Services
- **B2B / Employer offering** (/foretag/) — corporate health packages
- **Apotek Hjärtat / ICA partnership** — physical distribution at 60+ supermarket sites
- **Skandia / insurance integration** — mentioned as partner

## How It Serves Patients Better Than Traditional Care

| Problem with traditional Swedish care | Min Doktor's answer |
|---|---|
| Phone queues at 8am to get same-day GP appointment | 24/7 app, no phone, response in ~1 hour |
| Must be listed with a specific health center | Any Swedish resident can use digital service |
| Long waits for referrals | Digital referral issued in same chat session |
| Physical presence required for prescription renewal | Dedicated renewal flow, no visit needed |
| Vaccine tracking is a paper card you lose | Digital vaccination card in patient account |
| Children's care requires pediatric specialist | Children covered digitally from 6 months |
| Driving to clinic for minor infections | 100 SEK chat with doctor from home |

## What Min Doktor Lacks vs. Competitors

### vs. Kry / Livi
| Gap | Detail |
|---|---|
| **No live video consultation** | Min Doktor is async text only. Kry is video-first — patients who want face-to-face feel are underserved |
| **No real-time chat** | All responses are delayed (~1hr). Kry connects in minutes via live video |
| **No European presence** | Kry operates in Sweden, UK, France, Germany, Norway. Min Doktor is Sweden-only |
| **Weaker mental health product** | Kry has a dedicated mental health vertical with therapists. Min Doktor covers stress/burnout but no structured therapy programs |

### vs. Doktor.se
| Gap | Detail |
|---|---|
| **No real-time messaging** | Doktor.se offers live chat. Min Doktor does not |
| **No symptom checker / pre-triage AI** | Doktor.se and others have AI-assisted intake. Min Doktor uses static questionnaires |
| **No smart navigation / triage** | The 55-condition flat list (the problem Right Door solves) — no guided "I don't know what I have" path |

### vs. Zocdoc / Teladoc (International benchmark)
| Gap | Detail |
|---|---|
| **No doctor marketplace / ratings visible pre-booking** | Patients cannot choose or see their doctor before BankID login |
| **No estimated wait time shown pre-login** | Zero transparency on response time before committing |
| **No appointment scheduling UI** | No calendar picker, no slot booking — entirely async |
| **No patient health record summary** | No visible health timeline or visit history dashboard pre-login |
| **No wearable / device integration** | No Apple Health, Fitbit, Oura, or CGM data ingestion |

### vs. Neko Health / Sword Health (Design / Innovation benchmark)
| Gap | Detail |
|---|---|
| **No preventive health screening** | Neko offers full-body health scans + data-driven health scores. Min Doktor is reactive only |
| **No digital rehabilitation programmes** | Sword Health offers AI-guided physiotherapy. Min Doktor has a physiotherapist but no structured program |
| **No AI symptom routing** | Nabla, Sword, and others use LLMs for triage. Min Doktor uses static form routing |
| **No personalised health dashboard** | No "your health over time" view — only individual consultation records |

### Navigation & Product UX Gaps (The Right Door Problem)
| Gap | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Flat 55-condition list** — no grouping or smart filter | First-time patients don't know which condition to pick |
| **No "I don't know what I have" path** | Patients who can't self-diagnose have no guided route |
| **BankID hard wall on first click** | No preview of what happens after login — high drop-off |
| **No body-based navigation** | Patient must think in medical terms, not body sensations |
| **No estimated price shown on condition page** | Cost not visible until deep in the flow |
| **No live availability indicator** | No "doctors available now" status anywhere |
| **No doctor profile visible before login** | Trust building happens too late in the funnel |
| **Search autocomplete unconfirmed / weak** | Typing in the hero search may not yield live suggestions |
| **Mobile tab overflow** | "Weight loss" tab clips on 375px — not discoverable |

## Regulatory & Business Risks (Context for Prototype)

| Risk | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Region Sörmland contract dependency** | Entire digital care pricing model depends on one region contract |
| **2026 written warning** | IVO/Sörmland warned Min Doktor over Blodtrycksdoktorn subcontract — signals compliance complexity |
| **BankID lock-in** | Non-Swedish residents (tourists, immigrants without personnummer) pay 550 SEK and face UX friction |
| **Async-only model** | As video call expectations grow (post-COVID), async text may feel outdated |
